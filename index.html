How we work

Principles we build by

  • Community first We design for helpful, respectful participation, with moderation tools built in from day one.
  • Safe and private by default We collect only the data a feature needs and protect members' accounts and conversations.
  • AI that assists Our AI handles repetitive work so people can spend their time on decisions and on each other.
  • Open to everyone Free tiers wherever we can, and interfaces that meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
